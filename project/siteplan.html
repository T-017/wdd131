Travel, Tour, and Terrorize

For the project I am propsing a topic regarding torism in Japan considering that it is a common toursit destination often sought after for its rich culture, hisotry, and natural beauty that is often depicted, as well as misreprisented, by popculture and social media. The site will of course feature various TRAVEL SITES and tourist friendly LOCATIONS across Japan, but will also include a section regarding CUSTOMS and ETIQUETTE to help visitors better understand and respect the local culture during their stay.

The reason for choosing this subject is due to my personal interest in my heritage and my wife is from Japan, so she is often sharing her experiences with the differnt places, foods, customs, and foreigners there. This would act as a great way to combine my web development skills with my passion for Japanese culture, creating a resource that could be both informative and engaging for potential travelers.
